FreeKasir

Cashier app for small shops

A free Android point-of-sale app for food stalls, grocery shops, coffee shops, and clothing stores. It records sales, stock, and finances, and keeps working without internet.

  • Core cashier features free forever on one device
  • Cash, bank transfer, e-wallet, and QRIS
  • Receipts via WhatsApp or a Bluetooth printer
