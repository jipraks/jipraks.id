Questions people ask

Is Cuplik free?

The app is free to download and use. The only paid part is the AI that finds highlights and writes titles: use the built-in AI balance you top up with QRIS, or your own AI API key. Rendering runs on your computer.

What if I lose my AI balance?

The built-in AI balance is tied to an account key on your computer, with no email or password. Cuplik asks you to save a backup of that key before your first top up. If the key is lost, the balance cannot be recovered, so keep the backup safe. Top ups are not refundable.

What computer do I need?

Windows 10 version 1809 or later, 64-bit, at least 8 GB of RAM, and an internet connection. A supported graphics card makes rendering faster, but you don't need one.

Do I need a Repliz account?

No. Repliz is only used if you want to schedule posts from Cuplik. Without it, your clips stay on your computer and you can upload them yourself.

When will it be on the Microsoft Store?

Cuplik is being reviewed for the Microsoft Store. Once it passes, you can download it there for free from the button on this page.

Can I clip any video?

Make sure you have the rights to the videos you clip and post, such as your own videos or videos whose owners allow it. Cuplik adds a source credit to each clip, but that does not replace permission.
